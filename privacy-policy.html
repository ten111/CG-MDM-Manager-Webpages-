Privacy Policy

Application: CG MDM Manager (Android) • Last Updated: September 2026
Important Notice: CG MDM Manager is a mobile application developed by E Kosh Tech Solutions to help educational institutions and authorized school personnel maintain Mid-Day Meal (MDM) / PM POSHAN administrative records. This policy describes our commitment to respecting user privacy and handling information responsibly.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the CG MDM Manager mobile application and its associated website. Our goal is to provide a transparent, straightforward tool for managing school-level meal records, enrollment aggregates, and inventory figures digitally.

By installing or using CG MDM Manager, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the information practices described in this document.

2. Information We May Collect

CG MDM Manager is designed primarily as a local-first utility for schools. Depending on your use of the application's features, the types of information processed may include:

  • School administrative information entered by the user (such as School Name, UDISE code, district, block, and contact details).
  • Aggregate numerical data related to student enrollment, daily meal attendance, and beneficiary counts.
  • Names and designations of teaching or cooking staff entered for internal school management.
  • Daily and monthly stock registers, meal consumption records, and grocery inventory counts.
  • Application preference settings, such as language preference (Hindi or English) and 4-digit PIN security lock settings.

3. Information Provided by Users

All school, staff, and student count records maintained inside the application are entered directly by authorized school personnel (such as headmasters, nodal teachers, or administrators). Users are responsible for ensuring that all data entered into the application complies with their school’s local administrative guidelines.

4. Device and Technical Information

To operate correctly on Android devices, the application may access standard system-level capabilities, which may include:

  • Local Storage: Required to save your school's database, meal entries, and local backup files directly on your device.
  • Network Access: Required if the user initiates an optional Google Drive cloud backup or sends support communications.
  • Basic Device State: Standard Android operating system information necessary to display the user interface properly and ensure stability.

5. How Information Is Used

Information entered into CG MDM Manager is used exclusively to provide the core administrative functions requested by the user, including:

  • Calculating daily and monthly meal consumption, food grain utilization, and cooking cost summaries.
  • Displaying organized reports, attendance totals, and holiday schedules.
  • Maintaining a chronological Audit Trail of record modifications for transparency.
  • Allowing the user to export or backup school records to their own storage media or linked Google Drive account.
  • Responding to user support requests submitted via email.

6. Data Storage

By default, CG MDM Manager stores your application database locally on your device. When you enable the optional Google Drive Cloud Backup engine, your encrypted or packaged backup file is saved directly to your personal or school Google Drive storage under your own Google account credentials. We do not operate external third-party advertising or commercial data-mining servers.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and operational measures to safeguard information:

  • App PIN Lock: A 4-digit PIN lock option is provided in the app to prevent unauthorized access by third parties who may handle your mobile device.
  • Audit Trail Logs: The application records modification logs to assist administrators in reviewing who updated meal and stock entries.
  • Local-First Architecture: Because your data remains on your device or in your personal cloud backup, you maintain control over your school records.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize any school, teacher, or student data. Information is not shared with external commercial marketing entities. Data is only transferred when explicitly directed by you (for example, when you export a PDF/Excel report or sync to your Google Drive).

9. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with the following standard platform services:

  • Google Play Services & Google Drive API: Used when the user authorizes backup synchronization to their personal Google account. Such usage is governed by Google’s respective privacy policies.
  • Android System Services: Used to render typography, process file exports, and handle printing or sharing intents.

10. Children's Privacy

CG MDM Manager is a tool intended strictly for use by adult school personnel, teachers, and administrators. It does not provide an account creation interface for children or solicit personal contact details directly from minors. Any student enrollment numbers recorded within the application are maintained by teachers solely for meal allocation and official school record purposes.

11. Data Retention

Because data is retained locally on your mobile device, records remain in your custody until you choose to delete them, clear the application storage, or uninstall the application. You can create local backups or remove obsolete academic years at your discretion.

12. User Rights

Users maintain full control over the information entered into CG MDM Manager. You have the right and ability to:

  • View, edit, or delete any record directly through the application interface.
  • Export records in structured formats for external review or physical printing.
  • Disconnect or delete Google Drive backup archives from your own Google Drive dashboard at any time.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in application features, legal requirements, or best practices. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will indicate when revisions took effect. We encourage users to periodically review this page.
