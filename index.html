About the Application

About CG MDM Manager

CG MDM Manager is an Android application designed to simplify the digital management of school-level Mid-Day Meal (MDM) and PM POSHAN related activities in schools across Chhattisgarh.

Developed by E Kosh Tech Solutions, the application equips headmasters, teachers, and school administrators with a practical, mobile-friendly toolkit to replace fragmented registers with structured digital records.

  • School Information Management
  • Student & Enrollment Records
  • Teaching & Cooking Staff Details
  • Daily & Monthly Meal Entries
  • Attendance & Beneficiary Tracking
  • Holiday & Academic Calendar
  • Inventory & Grain Stock Records
  • Summary Reports & Monitoring

Key Capabilities

Designed for Everyday School Operations

Explore the core modules built into CG MDM Manager to keep school records clean, accessible, and structured.

School Management

Maintain essential school-related information, UDISE code, institution details, and administrative contacts digitally.

Student Information

Maintain class-wise student enrollment figures and beneficiary counts for accurate mid-day meal planning.

Staff Management

Organize teaching staff, cook-cum-helpers (रसोइया), and school coordinators involved in meal preparation.

Meal Management

Log daily meals served, menu items, quantity dispatched, and monthly consumption records with precision.

Attendance & Records

Simplify daily record maintenance by quickly capturing meal attendance numbers and tracking participation trends.

Holiday & Calendar

Manage government holidays, vacations, non-serving days, and exam schedules to keep meal calculations accurate.

Reports & Summaries

Keep important monthly and quarterly figures organized for easier review, official submissions, and audits.

Digital Records & Backup

Reduce reliance on paper registers. Benefit from Google Drive automated backup, local export, and full audit trail logs.

How It Works

Getting started with CG MDM Manager takes only three straightforward steps.

01

Install the App

Download and install CG MDM Manager on your Android smartphone or tablet from the official download link.

02

Enter Information

Set up your school profile, staff members, enrolled students, and input daily or monthly meal records into the app.

03

Manage Digitally

Access organized records, view audit logs, generate consolidated reports, and securely backup data to Google Drive anytime.
